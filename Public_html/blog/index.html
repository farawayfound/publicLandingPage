David Chui

Father, Husband, Veteran, Engineer. Curious mind always seeking my next adventure. Currently a Software Engineer at Spectrum.

I was born in Xi’an China, raised in Montreal Canada, went to High School in Texas, and graduated from University of Colorado at Denver with a B.S. in Finance and Information Systems. I served in the USAF as a structural engineer at Nellis AFB in Las Vegas, where I met my wife. Home base for us is now the front range of the Rocky Mountains in Colorado, where we are raising our child to be a little mountain man.
