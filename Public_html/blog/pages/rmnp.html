Thin Air

The sun sets behind the mountains every day in Denver, as if inviting me to chase it into the valleys. There are endless adventures to be had in the rockies, one could easily spend a lifetime exploring them.

Rocky Mountain National Park is an excellent sampler plate to everything the rockies have to offer. Crystal clear alpine lakes, 14 thousand feet peaks that caress the clouds, creeks that chime into the night are just some of the gifts of the mountains. The mountains are addicting, and climbing each one doesn't quench my thirst, but makes me hungry for more.
